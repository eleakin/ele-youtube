## Notes

- Replace [CHANNEL LINK] with the channel URL once the handle is set
  (youtube.com/@yourhandle) — then it never changes.
- Timestamps ("chapters") appear on the video's progress bar when the
  first one is 0:00 and there are 3+ — they measurably help retention
  and search.
- Keep 3 hashtags max — YouTube shows only the first 3 above the title.
- The disclaimer on Template B is worth keeping on ALL patient-facing
  health content — it protects you and signals professionalism.
